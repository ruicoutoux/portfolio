01 / Enterprise IA & Platform Architecture Delivered

Portfolios: Restructuring How Enterprises Organise at Scale

OutSystems' flat org-app model couldn't support how large enterprises actually work. I defined a new intermediary layer — Portfolios — restructuring the platform hierarchy from Organization → Apps to Organization → Portfolio → Stages → Apps. This required remodelling the core navigation, since some concepts (apps, pipelines) now lived inside portfolios while others (users, settings) remained at org level.

Key Trade-off: Introducing structural hierarchy mid-platform risked breaking existing mental models for all users — the navigation redesign had to make the new model feel inevitable, not learned.

How I Work

Philosophy

Core principles guiding design strategy and cross-functional leadership.

As software transitions from tools operated by humans to autonomous agents supervised by humans, UX shifts from click-path optimization to intent configuration, policy dry-runs, and state-diff verification. Designing for supervisory trust requires explicit failure boundaries and transparent recovery options.
Design leadership isn’t just producing Figma deliverables. It means writing product specs alongside PMs, evaluating architectural trade-offs with Principal Engineers, defining operational proxy metrics early, and staying accountable for business impact.
When direct telemetry is constrained by enterprise privacy or early-stage launches, value is proven through operational proxies: setup step reduction, task completion speed under usability testing, support ticket drops, and design system reuse scalability.
I actively leverage LLMs for research synthesis, edge-case generation, and rapid code prototyping. However, final design judgment, accessibility standards, user empathy, and ethical trade-offs remain strictly human-led.
