How I Work

I focus on rigorous and informed product decisions to drive great experiences.

01 / FRAME

Clear problem understanding and framing

Before designing anything, I identify what's actually broken, for whom, and the outcomes we aim to achieve. The problem statement is the most important design artefact.

02 / SYSTEMS

Understand the whole system

I map the full product surface before touching a single screen, including APIs, permissions, data contracts, navigation dependencies, and organisational boundaries. Changes that look local rarely are.

03 / CONSTRAINTS

Close work with PM, Architecture & Engineering to establish constraints

Constraints are defined at the beginning. Working directly with PMs, architects, and engineers from the start means trade-offs are made consciously, not discovered after a design is finished.

04 / FORKS

Evaluate trade-offs

Every meaningful design decision has a cost. I map alternatives explicitly, align stakeholders on what each gives up, and make the chosen path defensible, not just aesthetically, but strategically.

05 / ABSTRACT

Abstract complexity

Complex systems don't need to feel complex. I translate layered technical realities, such as state machines, permission models, agentic flows, into interactions that feel inevitable to the user.

06 / VALIDATE

Iterate and Validate Fast

I use AI-assisted prototyping to compress the gap between concept and evidence. Early, low-cost validation, through rapid prototypes, usability tests, and proxy metrics, means bad ideas fail before they ship.
